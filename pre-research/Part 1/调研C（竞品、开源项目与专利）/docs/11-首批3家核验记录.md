## E001 · Oracle Payables Implementation Guide

- **来源类型**：官方文档（A）
- **链接**：https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E18727_01/doc.121/e12795/T434884T434889.htm
- **版本**：Oracle Payables 12.1 Implementation Guide
- **原文摘录**：
  > You can set options and tolerances in Payables and Purchasing to control matching in your system.
  >
  > In the Invoice Tolerance window you can set tolerances for the variance you will allow between invoices and purchase orders or receipts.
  >
  > Match Approval Level (2-way, 3-way, 4-way) Purchasing Options window

  在同一页的匹配异常清单中还列有：

  > Duplicate Invoice Number　Duplicate Line Number

- **覆盖能力**：三方匹配、容差、重复发票号/行号检查、未匹配暂挂
- **判定**：
  - 三方匹配 = `支持`
  - 容差配置 = `支持`（按发票与 PO/收货之间的差异设置）
  - 差异分类 = `部分`（文档按价格/数量差异设容差，但未展示业务分类界面）
  - 人工复核 = `部分`（Hold Unmatched Invoices + 审批，未见完整复核工作台）
- **局限**：12.1 版本较旧；不涉及中国数电票与电子凭证标准

## E002 · Oracle Procurement 25D · Match Approval Level Options

- **来源类型**：官方文档（A）
- **链接**：https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/procurement/25d/oapro/match-approval-level-options.html
- **原文摘录**：
  > Two-Way：Purchase order and invoice quantities must match within tolerance before the corresponding invoice can be paid.
  >
  > Three-Way：Purchase order, receipt, and invoice quantities must match within tolerance before the corresponding invoice can be paid.
  >
  > Four-Way：Purchase order, receipt, accepted quantities from inspection, and invoice quantities must match within tolerance before the corresponding invoice can be paid.

- **判定**：二方/三方/四方匹配 = `支持`；"within tolerance" 再次印证容差是匹配的前置条件
- **局限**：该页只定义匹配层级，不含容差具体配置与异常处理界面

## E003 · Medius · What is Invoice Matching?

- **来源类型**：厂商自述（B）
- **链接**：https://www.medius.com/glossary/what-is-invoice-matching/
- **原文摘录**：
  > Invoice matching involves comparing and linking a supplier invoice with the underlying data on which the cost is based – a purchase order and goods delivery receipt (GDR), or a contract.
  >
  > Three-way matching, a key invoice matching process in AP automation, ensures invoices are verified against POs and delivery receipts for added control.
  >
  > Understanding the differences between two-way, three-way, and four-way matching is critical for AP teams adopting automation.

- **判定**：三方匹配 = `支持`（厂商自述）；两方/四方同为 `支持`（同一段自述）
- **局限**：未提容差、差异分类、复核工作台；属营销/术语页，非产品文档
